TAPADIA CITY CENTRE (TCC) MALL

Tapadia City Centre (TCC) Mall is Amravati's premier shopping, dining, and entertainment destination, proudly serving as Western Vidarbha’s first complete modern mall. Opened in 2022, TCC brought a metro-city lifestyle experience directly to the heart of Amravati, making it a favorite hangout spot for families, youth, and shoppers from across the region.

Designed as a one-stop hub for the community, the mall perfectly balances retail, food, and fun under a single roof.
